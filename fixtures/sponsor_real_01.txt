New Belgium Brewing Company announces its FY2026 Bicycle Advocacy Grant Program. 
This grant supports grassroots efforts that increase bicycle ridership and improve equity, safety, and access in communities. 
The grant range is $500 to $20,000 per organization. 
Applications for the FY2026 cycle are currently open and the deadline for submission is March 31, 2026. 
Eligible applicants must be 501(c)3 organizations. 
For more details and to apply, visit https://grantstation.com/grantmakers/new-belgium-bicycle-advocacy-grant or contact the grants committee at grants@newbelgium.com.
