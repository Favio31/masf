Global Adventure Sports Foundation (GASF) announces its 2026 Multi-Tier Support Program for independent cyclists, hikers, and expedition athletes.

The program offers three levels of financial support:

TIER 1 — Explorer Grant
Amount: €15,000
Deadline: October 15, 2026
Eligibility: Solo cyclists or hikers planning expeditions of 30+ days in remote regions.
Apply at: https://gasf.example.org/explorer-grant
Contact: explorer@gasf.example.org

TIER 2 — Expedition Grant
Amount: €20,000
Deadline: October 30, 2026
Eligibility: Small teams (2-4 people) undertaking multi-month expeditions with documented community impact.
Apply at: https://gasf.example.org/expedition-grant
Contact: expedition@gasf.example.org

TIER 3 — Legacy Grant
Amount: €50,000
Deadline: November 20, 2026
Eligibility: Established athletes or organizations with 5+ years of documented work in underserved regions, proposing scalable community projects.
Apply at: https://gasf.example.org/legacy-grant
Contact: legacy@gasf.example.org

All tiers require a detailed expedition plan, budget breakdown, and proof of prior outdoor experience. Decisions will be announced 30 days after each deadline.
